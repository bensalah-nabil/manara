Manara

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026. Applies to the Manara app for Android and the Manara parent console.

Manara lets a parent set rules on their own child's phone. The phone always shows that it is managed and what is shared; Manara has no hidden mode. Manara never collects messages, photos, contacts, what is said on a call, browsing history or passwords. Call history is optional, is off by default, and exists only in the version of Manara installed from outside Google Play.

Who is responsible

Manara is run by Nabil Ben Salah, in Tunisia. Questions, requests to see or delete data, and complaints: nabil.bsalah0@gmail.com.

A parent who connects a phone decides what is collected about the child using it, and confirms when connecting it that they are the child's parent or legal guardian, that the child is a minor, and that the child will be told the phone is managed. Manara processes the child's data on that parent's behalf, for that purpose only.

What is not collected

Messages, emails, photos, videos, files, contacts, call audio, microphone or camera, browsing history, what is typed, and passwords. The Google Play version never reads calls. In the version installed from outside Google Play, a parent can turn on call history: the number, direction, time and length of each call from that moment on, shown on the child's phone while it is on and deleted after 30 days. The other person in a call is not told. If the parent also turns on notification access on the child's phone, calls in WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram and Viber are noted from their call notifications: which app, when and roughly how long, never who called. Manara looks at notifications only to spot those calls and keeps nothing else from them. Website filtering works by pointing the phone at a family-safe DNS service the parent chooses (for example AdGuard Family); that service, not Manara, answers the phone's DNS lookups, under its own privacy policy.

Who processes it

  • Render (server hosting, Frankfurt, Germany) and Neon (database, Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Netlify (hosts this website and the parent console).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, to wake the phone when the parent sends a command or changes a rule. The message carries no data, only "check in now".
  • Brevo (email), to send the parent a password-reset link and alerts, such as a phone that stopped checking in. It receives the parent's email address and the email's text, never the child's data.
  • Google Play services on the phone, for location and for scanning the connection QR code.

The server and database are in Germany, so data is sent from the phone and the console to the European Union. Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

How it is protected

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and device tokens as hashes. On the phone, its token and release key are encrypted with the Android Keystore. Only the parent account a phone belongs to can see its data.

The child's side

The phone always shows a "This phone is managed" notice, and a separate notice while location is shared. Opening Manara on the phone shows exactly what the parent can see and which rules are on. Only the parent can release the phone, from the console or with a release code.

Your choices and rights

A parent can turn any collection off at any time, delete saved account lists, remove a phone, which deletes its data, and delete the whole parent account from the console (how to delete your account). To see, correct or delete any data, write to nabil.bsalah0@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority: in Tunisia, the Instance Nationale de Protection des Données Personnelles (INPDP).
