Manara

Delete your Manara account

For parents who use Manara, the parental controls app by Nabil Ben Salah.

Delete it yourself, in the console

  1. Release every phone first: in the console, open each phone, then the Phone tab, and choose Remove Manara from this phone. This takes Manara off the phone and deletes that phone's data. If the phone can't be reached, Show release code gives a code to type on it; after that, choose Remove from my list. If a phone is lost or was reset, choose Remove from my list instead.
  2. Choose Account at the top of the console, then Delete your account.
  3. Enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

The console asks you to release every phone first on purpose: a child's phone still managed by an account that no longer exists could not be released by anyone.

What is deleted

Everything stored with the account, at once: your email address and password hash, and for every phone its rules, activity reports, locations, app usage, limit history, protection status, setup records, and its push token, device token and release key. Nothing is kept for any other purpose.

The database provider keeps a short restore history, 6 hours on the current plan, after which deleted data is gone for good. The server's logs hold only internal account and phone numbers, never your email or your child's data. If you received emails from Manara, our email provider (Brevo) keeps its own record of them (address and subject) for its limited retention period.

If you can't sign in

Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email. If that doesn't work, write to nabil.bsalah0@gmail.com from the email address of the account, and ask for it to be deleted. We answer within 30 days, and we will ask you to release the phones first, or confirm they are lost or reset.
