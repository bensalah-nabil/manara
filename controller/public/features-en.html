What Manara does

Three one-minute videos to see how Manara protects your child. Nothing hidden: your child always sees what is on.

Video 1 / 3

At night, apps sleep

  • You choose bedtime.
  • TikTok, YouTube and games pause.
  • Calls, texts, WhatsApp and the Quran stay open.
  • In the morning, it all comes back by itself.
Video 2 / 3

A safer internet

  • Adult sites are blocked, in Chrome and in apps.
  • Google search stays on SafeSearch.
  • Optional: gambling, dating and alcohol sites blocked in Chrome.
  • Other browsers are closed.
Video 3 / 3

Strangers who call

  • You write down the numbers you know.
  • Another or a hidden number calls? You get an alert.
  • The call is marked in the history.
  • Manara never listens to calls.

On Android, with the version downloaded from the website.

And also

⏱️A daily time for social media, and one for gamesTikTok, Facebook and Instagram share one budget.
🔒The rules stay onNo reset, safe mode or clock change to get around them.

📍Location, if you chooseThe phone shows a notice while it is on.
📲Apps installed or removedTheir name and the time, never what is inside.
✉️A summary every MondayThe week’s screen time and the most used apps.

Manara never reads messages, never sees photos and never listens to calls. Your child’s phone always shows that it is protected.
