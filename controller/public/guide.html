How Manara works

Manara manages your child's Android phone openly. The phone always shows a notice saying it is managed, and opening Manara on it shows exactly which rules are on and what you can see. There is no hidden mode. Manara never reads messages, photos, contacts, calls or browsing history.

You set the rules in the console, a web page you open on your own phone or computer. Manara on the child's phone applies them, and keeps applying them without a connection.

Before you start

Setup takes about 15 minutes. It removes every account from the phone for a few minutes, so prepare:

  • The phone runs Android 11 or newer, is on Wi-Fi, and is charged above 50%. No computer or cable is needed.
  • Back up chats (for WhatsApp: Settings, Chats, Chat backup). Apps removed during setup lose what is stored only on the phone.
  • Know the password of every account on the phone (Google, Samsung or Xiaomi, and each app), or have them written down. Manara never sees or stores passwords.
  • On a Samsung, a Secure Folder must be removed first. Move anything you need out of it.
  • Tell your child the phone will be managed, and why.

1. Create your account

Open the console, choose New here? Create an account, and enter your email address and a password of at least 8 characters. Use an address you check: password-reset links and alerts go there.

2. Connect the phone

  1. Install Manara on your child's phone.
  2. In the console, choose + Add a phone. Confirm the three statements (you are the parent or guardian, the child is a minor, the child will be told), and pick the phone's brand.
  3. Follow the console's steps for that brand to turn on Wireless debugging: usually Settings, About phone, tap Build number seven times, then Developer options, Wireless debugging. If a menu name differs, search for "Build number" in Settings.
  4. Tick each item of the checklist. The console shows a connection code, as a QR code and as text.
  5. On the phone, open Manara and tap Scan the code, or type it.

3. Guided setup

  1. On the phone, tap Start guided setup in Manara, then open Wireless debugging (Manara's Open Developer options button gets you there). Leave the phone on that screen: Android stops pairing the moment you leave it.
  2. In the console, open the phone's page. It offers two ways to pair:
    • QR code: on the phone, tap Pair device with QR code and scan the QR code shown in the console.
    • Pairing code: on the phone, tap Pair device with pairing code, and type the 6-digit code the phone shows into the console.
  3. Setup then runs by itself, and the console shows each step. Manara pauses the apps that hold an account (Google, WhatsApp...) for a few seconds, becomes the phone's manager, and turns them back on. No app is uninstalled and no app data is lost.
  4. On some phones (Samsung, for example) Android also needs the accounts themselves removed for a moment. The console then shows how you'll get back in to each one without remembering a password (a passkey on your phone, an SMS code, or Family Link), and asks you to confirm first. Then tap Open accounts in Manara's notice on the phone and remove them. Don't uninstall any app.
  5. At the end, tap Add Google account in Manara's notice and sign back in. Apps like Facebook usually come back by themselves. Then choose Finish setup in the console.

Once setup has finished, Manara blocks developer options by default, so a computer can't be used to undo the rules. You can change this under Rules, Settings restrictions.

4. Choose the rules

Open the phone in the console. Every rule is off until you turn it on, and the child sees each one on the phone.

Rules tab

  • App time limits: daily minutes for the apps you choose. One limit can be shared by several apps. 0 minutes blocks an app.
  • Lock schedule: hours when the phone stays locked, such as bedtime or school, per day of the week.
  • Site filtering: sends all website lookups through a family-safe filtering service (a Private DNS host). Also block Private DNS and VPN settings under Settings restrictions, so it can't be turned off.
  • Chrome: SafeSearch on Google, YouTube Restricted Mode, a list of websites to block, and turning off Chrome's own secure DNS so the site filter applies. These work in Chrome only. Block another browser with a 0-minute app limit.
  • Settings restrictions: stop the child resetting the phone, using safe mode, adding users, changing VPN or Private DNS, or using developer options.

Sharing tab

  • Location: the phone sends its location at the interval you choose, and shows a notice while it does.
  • Daily usage report: minutes per app today, sent about once an hour.

Phone tab

  • Name and color: your child's name and a color, so each phone is easy to find in your list.

A saved rule reaches the phone within seconds. The console says when it can't, and when the phone will get it instead.

5. Every day

  • Lock now, at the top of the phone's page, locks the phone in a few seconds.
  • The Activity tab shows whether every protection is working, daily app usage if you turned it on, and when a time limit was reached.
  • The list of phones shows when each phone last checked in.

6. Alerts by email

Manara emails you when:

  • a phone hasn't checked in for 12 hours: it may be off, out of battery, or offline, or Manara may have been stopped;
  • a protection stops working, for example Usage access turned off, or Private DNS changed.

You get one email per problem, not one every check-in. Check your spam folder the first time.

7. Move or remove Manara

Only you can do this. Manara on the phone has no button for it, so your child can't remove it.

  • Remove Manara from this phone (Phone tab): within seconds, or at the phone's next check-in if it is offline, every rule is lifted and the phone becomes an ordinary phone again.
  • Move to another account: for example, to the other parent's account. The phone keeps its setup.
  • Phone can't be reached? Choose Show release code and type the code on the phone, in Manara, under Release this phone. Then choose Remove from my list.
  • A lost or reset phone: Remove from my list.

To delete your account and all its data, see Delete your account.

If something goes wrong

Setup stays on "pairing"

The phone must stay on the Wireless debugging screen until pairing is done. Open it again, choose the QR code or the pairing code again, and try once more. The phone and the console must both be online.

The phone shows "Not reporting"

The phone hasn't checked in for 2 hours. Check that it is on and online. Opening Manara on it and tapping Check now makes it check in at once. On some phones, battery saving delays apps in the background: allow Manara to run without restrictions in the phone's battery settings.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot your password?. The emailed link works for one hour.
